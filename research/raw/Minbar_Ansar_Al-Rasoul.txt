The '''Minbar Ansar al-Rasoul''' ({{Langx|Ar|منبر أنصار الرسول|4=Prophet's Supporters Platform}}) is a militant [[Sunni Islam|Sunni]] [[Islamism|Islamist]] organization in [[Jaramana]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=الصحة السورية تعلن مقتل شخصين في انفجار عبوة ناسفة بحافلة في جرمانا بريف دمشق (موسع) |url=https://arabic.news.cn/20260807/2a0847baaae24fc1b3e4c8a98ae24ed1/c.html |access-date=2026-08-06 |website=arabic.news.cn}}</ref>

==History==
===Foundation===
On July 13, 2025, the group published threatening messages to the Christian and Druze communities of [[Jaramana]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Minbar Ansar al-Rasoul claims deadly Damascus bus bombing |url=https://shafaq.com/en/Middle-East/Minbar-Ansar-al-Rasoul-claims-deadly-Damascus-bus-bombing |access-date=2026-08-06 |website=Shafaq News {{!}} Latest breaking news in Iraq and the world |language=en}}</ref> 

===Activities===
On July 15, 2025, the group celebrated the "victory" of the [[Internal Security Command (Syria)|Internal Security Forces]] over the Druze protesters in Jaramana.<ref name=":0">{{Cite web |date=August 6, 2026 |title=من هي جماعة "منبر أنصار الرسول" التي تبنّت تفجير جرمانا في ريف دمشق؟ |url=https://www.annahar.com/arab-world/arabian-levant/336471/%D9%85%D9%86-%D9%87%D9%8A-%D8%AC%D9%85%D8%A7%D8%B9%D8%A9-%D9%85%D9%86%D8%A8%D8%B1-%D8%A3%D9%86%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B1%D8%B3%D9%88%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D9%8A-%D8%AA%D8%A8%D9%86%D8%AA-%D8%AA%D9%81%D8%AC%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%AC%D8%B1%D9%85%D8%A7%D9%86%D8%A7-%D9%81%D9%8A-%D8%B1%D9%8A%D9%81-%D8%AF%D9%85%D8%B4%D9%82 |access-date=August 7, 2026|website=[[An-Nahar]]}}</ref>

On August 2, 2026, the group claimed to have a "cyber arm" that collects information on citizens of Jaramana and threatened to attack Jaramana.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Alabi |first=Rizik |date=August 9, 2026 |title=Obscure Group Claims Responsibility for Jaramana Bus Bombing That Killed 2 and Injured at Least 13 |url=https://themedialine.org/headlines/obscure-group-claims-responsibility-for-jaramana-bus-bombing-that-killed-2-and-injured-at-least-13/ |access-date=August 19, 2026 |website=[[The Media Line]]}}</ref>
