{{Politics of Syria}}
The '''Syrian transitional government''',{{efn|{{langx|ar|الحكومة الانتقالية السورية|al-Ḥukūmah al-Intiqāliyyah as-Sūriyyah}}}} also known as the '''Ahmed al-Sharaa government''', is the current provisional government of Syria. It was established on 29 March 2025 under President Ahmed al-Sharaa and is set to serve for a five-year transitional period under the [[Constitutional Declaration of the Syrian Arab Republic|Constitutional Declaration]], which establishes a  [[presidential system]]. As president, al-Sharaa has the authority to appoint and dismiss ministers and accept their resignations. The government succeeded the [[Syrian caretaker government]], which was headed by [[Mohammed al-Bashir]] during the period when al-Sharaa served as Syria's de facto leader and held authority over the caretaker government before his appointment as president.<ref name="UN Heads pdf">{{cite web|url=https://www.un.org/dgacm/sites/www.un.org.dgacm/files/Documents_Protocol/hspmfmlist.pdf|title=HEADS OF STATE HEADS OF GOVERNMENT MINISTERS FOR FOREIGN AFFAIRS|publisher=United Nations|access-date=}}</ref>{{Ahmed al-Sharaa series}}

The government was announced by Ahmed al-Sharaa at a ceremony at the [[People's Palace (Syria)|People's Palace]] in [[Damascus]], where the new ministers were sworn in and delivered speeches outlining their agendas. Two new ministries were formed: the [[Ministry of Sports and Youth (Syria)|Ministry of Sports and Youth]] and [[Ministry of Emergency and Disaster Management]]. The position of [[Prime Minister of Syria|Prime Minister]] was abolished. The [[Ministry of Energy (Syria)|Ministry of Energy]] was formed from the mergers of the [[Ministry of Electricity (Syria)|Ministry of Electricity]], the [[Ministry of Oil and Mineral Resources (Syria)|Ministry of Oil and Mineral Resources]] and the [[Ministry of Water Resources (Syria)|Ministry of Water Resources]],<ref>{{Cite web |last=Lister |first=Charles |date=30 March 2025 |title=Assessing Syria's Transitional Government |url=https://www.syriaweekly.com/p/assessing-syrias-transitional-government |access-date=31 March 2025 |website=Syria Weekly ([[Substack]]) |quote=In forming the new transitional government, Ahmed al-Sharaa has folded in Syria's long-standing ministerial positions responsible for electricity, oil and gas into the Minister of Energy's portfolio}}{{subscription required}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |date=29 March 2025 |title=دمج وزارتي الكهرباء والنفط.. الرئيس السوري يُعلن عن إصلاحات شاملة للقطاع |trans-title=Merger of the Ministries of Electricity and Oil.. ==Background==
The [[2024 Syrian opposition offensives]], codenamed "Deterrence of Aggression", were led by [[Hay'at Tahrir al-Sham]] and supported by allied [[Turkey|Turkish]]-backed groups in the [[Syrian National Army]]. These offensives resulted in the rapid [[Fall of the Assad regime|fall]] of [[Bashar al-Assad]]'s [[Ba'athist Syria|dictatorship]], ending five decades of [[Assad family]] rule that began when [[Hafez al-Assad]] assumed power in 1970 under the [[Ba'ath Party (Syrian-dominated faction)|Ba'ath Party]] following a [[Corrective Movement (Syria)|coup d'état]].<ref>{{Cite web |last1=Gebeily |first1=Maya |last2=Azhari |first2=Timour |date=8 December 2024 |access-date=7 April 2025 |title=Assad gets asylum in Russia, rebels sweep through Syria |url=https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/syria-rebels-celebrate-captured-homs-set-sights-damascus-2024-12-07/ |url-access=registration |url-status=live |archive-url=https://archive.today/20241218174033/https://www.reuters.com/world/middle-east/syria-rebels-celebrate-captured-homs-set-sights-damascus-2024-12-07/ |archive-date=18 December 2024 |website=[[Reuters]]}}</ref>

al-Sharaa]] until the formation of the [[Syrian caretaker government|caretaker government]], which was led by [[Mohammed al-Bashir]].<ref>{{cite web |date=8 December 2024 |title=الجولاني: المؤسسات ==Formation==
[[File:Syrian President decrees 9 29-03-2025.pdf|thumb|Presidential Decree No. 9 of 2025, forming the transitional government]]
The government was announced by Syrian president [[Ahmed al-Sharaa]] at a ceremony at the [[Presidential Palace, Damascus|Presidential Palace]] in [[Damascus]],<ref>{{Cite web |date=30 March 2025 |title=President al-Sharaa announces formation of the New Government: We are witnessing the birth of a new phase in our national process |url=https://www.sana.sy/en/?p=350809 |access-date=29 March 2025 |website=[[Syrian Arab News Agency]]}}</ref> in which the new ministers were sworn in and delivered speeches outlining their agendas.<ref>{{Cite web |date=29 March 2025 |title=وزراء الحكومة السورية يقدمون خططهم ويؤدون القسم الدستوري |trans-title=Syrian government ministers present their plans and take the constitutional oath |url=https://www.syria.tv/وزراء-الحكومة-السورية-يقدمون-خططهم-ويؤدون-القسم-الدستوري |access-date=29 March 2025 |website=[[Syria TV (Fadaat Media)|Syria TV]] |language=ar}}</ref> Two new ministries were formed: the [[Ministry of Sports and Youth (Syria)|Ministry of Sports and Youth]] and [[Ministry of Emergency and Disaster Management]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=نص الإعلان الدستوري لسوريا 2025 |trans-title=Text of the Constitutional Declaration of Syria 2025 |url=https://www.aljazeera.net/encyclopedia/2025/3/14/نص-الإعلان-الدستوري-لسوريا-2025 |date=14 March 2025 |access-date=29 March 2025 |website=[[Al Jazeera Arabic]] |language=ar |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250314135843/https://www.aljazeera.net/encyclopedia/2025/3/14/نص-الإعلان-الدستوري-لسوريا-2025 |archive-date=14 March 2025 |url-status=live}}</ref> The post of prime minister was abolished.<ref name=":2">{{Cite web |title=Syrian president unveils transitional government |url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/3/30/syrian-president-unveils-transitional-government |date=30 March 2025 |access-date=30 March 2025 |website=[[Al Jazeera English]] |language=en}}</ref>

Four of the new ministers belonged to minority ethnic groups or religions: [[Yaarub Bader]], an [[Alawites|Alawite]]; [[Amjad Badr]], a [[Druze]]; [[Hind Kabawat]], a [[Christians|Christian]]; and [[Mohammad Abdul Rahman Tarkou]], a [[Kurds in Syria|Kurd]].<ref name=":2"/> The government also included figures from different groups of the former [[Syrian opposition (2011–2024)|Syrian opposition]]: during the [[Syrian civil war|civil war]], [[Mohammed Abu al-Khair Shukri]] was a member of the [[National Coalition of Syrian Revolutionary and Opposition Forces|Syrian National Coalition]], [[Raed al-Saleh]] was director of the [[White Helmets (Syrian civil war)|White Helmets]] and Hind Kabawat served as deputy head of the [[Syrian Negotiation Commission]] in Geneva.<ref>{{cite web |title=Syrian Ambassador to UAE defects |url=https://gulfnews.com/world/mena/syrian-ambassador-to-uae-defects-1.1053545 |website=[[Gulf News]] |date=15 September 2018 |access-date=7 April 2025}}</ref> The new government was described by some observers as technocratic, with "ministers chosen according to their competences" by President al-Sharaa.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.dw.com/en/is-syrias-new-technocratic-cabinet-as-inclusive-as-it-could-be/a-72100412|title=How inclusive is Syria's new technocratic cabinet?|first=Cathrin|last=Schaer|publisher=DW|date=31 March 2025|accessdate=19 May 2025}}</ref> 

Al-Sharaa also retained [[Asaad al-Shaibani]] as [[Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates (Syria)|Minister of Foreign Affairs and Expatriates]] and [[Murhaf Abu Qasra]] as [[Ministry of Defense (Syria)|Minister of Defense]].<ref>{{Cite web |date=30 March 2025 |title=President al-Sharaa announces formation of the New Government: We are witnessing the birth of a new phase in our national process |url=https://www.sana.sy/en/?p=350809 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20250330132130/https://www.sana.sy/en/?p=350809 |archive-date=30 March 2025 |access-date=2 April 2025 |publisher=[[Syrian Arab News Agency]]}}</ref>

== Policies ==

===Possible reforms===
[[President of Syria|Syrian president]] [[Ahmed al-Sharaa]] stated that the new government will reform the energy sector for sustainability and reliable electricity, support farmers to ensure food production, revive the industry, protect national products, attract investment, stabilize the economy, strengthen the [[Syrian pound]], and prevent financial manipulation.<ref name=":1" />

On 16 April, 2025, Syria's minister of the interior, [[Anas Khattab]], announced that government forces had successfully thwarted an attempted coup by officers loyal to the old regime and that the government would create mechanisms to identify those responsible.<ref name="npasyria">{{Cite web |last=Roj |first=Kardo |date=16 April 2025 |title=Syrian Authorities Foil Alleged Coup Plot by Former Regime Officers |url=https://npasyria.com/en/124538/ |access-date=18 April 2025 |website=NPA Syria}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Coup plot by former regime officers foiled- Syrian interior minister |url=https://jordandaily.net/syrian-interior-minister-says-coup-plot-by-former-regime-officers-foiled/ |access-date=18 April 2025 |website=Jordan Daily}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Syrian Interior Minister says it thwarted coup attempt |url=https://akipress.com/news:828154:Syrian_Interior_Minister_says_it_thwarted_coup_attempt/ |access-date=18 April 2025 |website=[[AKIpress News Agency]]}}</ref> The announcement came as part of an increased network of collaboration between the new various security apparatus, from police to military to intelligence.<ref>{{Cite web |date=17 April 2025 |title=Syria thwarts coup plot by former regime officers |url=https://www.middleeastmonitor.com/20250417-syria-thwarts-coup-plot-by-former-regime-officers/ |access-date=18 April 2025 |website=[[Middle East Monitor]]}}</ref> According to Khattab, security forces had been tipped off to the plot, and were able to launch an operation to disrupt it before it could be executed, although exact details of who specifically was involved are scarce.<ref name="npasyria" />

===Domestic policies===
In January 2026, al-Sharaa declared a ceasefire between government forces and the [[Syrian Democratic Forces]]. He also recognised [[Kurdish language|Kurdish]] as one of Syria's national languages and announced the restoration of citizenship to [[Kurds in Syria|Kurdish Syrians]]. That same month, interior minister [[Anas Khattab]] ordered implementation of the citizenship measure, including for [[Kurds]] registered as stateless. The ministry set 5 February as the deadline for the implementation. The measure also directed the state to protect Kurdish culture and language in Syria, and to support Kurdish-language instruction in public and private schools in Kurdish-majority areas.<ref name=graimm>{{cite web | title=Syria grants immediate citizenship to Kurds in wake of gains against SDF | website=Al Jazeera | date=2026-01-29 | url=https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/1/29/syria-grants-immediate-citizenship-to-kurds-in-wake-of-gains-against-sdf | access-date=2026-07-04}}</ref>
