'''Shadi Mohammed al-Waisi'''{{efn|{{langx|ar|شادي محمد الويسي}}; born 1985}} is a Syrian politician and former rebel leader who served as the Minister of Justice in the [[Syrian caretaker government]] from December 2024 to March 2025.<ref name="AlJazeeraTransition">{{cite web |url=https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2024/12/10/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A |title=الحكومة السورية الانتقالية برئاسة البشير تتسلم مقاليد السلطة في دمشق |trans-title=The Syrian Transitional Government headed by al-Bashir assumes power in Damascus |website=Al Jazeera |language=ar |date=10 December 2024 |access-date=11 December 2024 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241210114842/https://www.aljazeera.net/news/2024/12/10/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%88%D8%B1%D9%8A%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%AA%D9%85%D8%B9-%D8%A8%D8%AD%D8%B6%D9%88%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%8A |archive-date=10 December 2024 |url-status=live }}</ref> He had also served as Minister of Justice in the [[Syrian Salvation Government]] during its sixth term, starting in 2022 and up until the December 2024 [[fall of the Assad regime]].<ref name="SSGJustice">{{cite web |url=https://syriansg.org/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84/ |title=حكومة الإنقاذ السورية: وزير العدل |trans-title=Syrian Salvation Government: Minister of Justice |website=Syrian Salvation Government |access-date=10 December 2024 |language=ar |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20241209010501/https://syriansg.org/%D9%88%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%B1-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B9%D8%AF%D9%84/ |archive-date=9 December 2024 |url-status=dead }}</ref>

== Education ==
Al-Waisi holds a degree in [[Sharia|Islamic Sharia]] and a diploma in educational qualification. He is also working on a master's [[thesis]] in Islamic and judicial studies. Prior to his judicial and political career, he worked as an Islamic education teacher and served as an [[imam]] and preacher in [[Aleppo]] for seven years.<ref name="SSGJustice"/>

=== Syrian Salvation Government ===
During the [[Syrian Civil War]], he played a role in the establishment of judicial institutions in areas under the control of opposition factions. He co-founded the Sharia Authority in Dhahret Awad and served as a judge. Later, he joined the founding cadre of the Quadripartite Sharia Authority in Aleppo, where he held positions as a military misdemeanor judge, an appeals judge, and the [[Prosecutor|public prosecutor]].<ref name="SSGJustice"/>

Al-Waisi also founded and presided over the Judicial Circuit Project and established key judicial institutions, including the Salqin Court, Hreitan Court, and the Northern Badiya Court. In addition to these roles, he has served as the head of the Civil Felonies Court and the Criminal Appeals Court under the [[Syrian Salvation Government]]. He is also a member of the Supreme Judicial Council.<ref name="SSGJustice"/>

== Minister of Justice ==
In 2024, al-Waisi began serving as the Minister of Justice in the [[Syrian caretaker government]] under prime minister [[Mohammed al-Bashir]] after the [[fall of the Assad regime]] and the decision that ministers of the salvation government will serve in the same roles in the transitional government until March 2025.<ref name="AlJazeeraTransition"/>
